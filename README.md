# Content Writer

Research-based content writing partner. Helps you research, outline, draft, and refine content while maintaining your unique voice.

## Overview

Specialized partner for creating research-driven content with citations. Unlike doc-coauthoring (structured documents), this skill focuses on:
- Blog posts and articles
- Newsletters and thought leadership
- Case studies and tutorials
- Content with sources and citations

## Workflow

```
Understand --> Outline --> Research --> Draft --> Feedback --> Polish
    ^                                    |
    +-------- iterate per section -------+
```

## Phase 1: Understand

Clarify the project:
- **Topic & thesis** — What's the main argument?
- **Audience** — Who reads this? What do they already know?
- **Format** — Blog post, newsletter, tutorial, case study?
- **Goal** — Educate, persuade, entertain, explain?
- **Length** — Short (500-800), medium (1000-2000), long (2500+)?
- **Style** — Formal, conversational, technical, storytelling?

## Phase 2: Outline

Build structure collaboratively:

```markdown
# [Working Title]

## Hook: [Opening angle]
## Introduction: Context, problem
## Section 1: [Title] -- [RESEARCH NEEDED: ...]
## Section 2: [Title] -- [RESEARCH NEEDED: ...]
## Conclusion: Summary, CTA
```

Mark research gaps inline.

## Phase 3: Research

For each gap:
1. Search for relevant, recent, credible sources
2. Extract key facts, data, quotes
3. Record full citations immediately
4. Add findings to outline with markers

Present findings with full citations and location in the piece.

## Phase 4: Draft

Write **one section at a time**, pause for feedback:
1. Write section based on outline and research
2. Include citations for claims needing support
3. Get review before moving to next section

## Phase 5: Section Feedback

As each section drafts, review:
- **What Works** — 2-3 strengths
- **Suggestions** — Clarity, flow, evidence, style issues
- **Line Edits** — 1-3 most impactful changes
- **Questions** — 1-2 questions that deepen thinking

## Phase 6: Polish & Final Review

When complete:
- [ ] All claims have citations
- [ ] Citations formatted consistently
- [ ] Hook is compelling
- [ ] Transitions are smooth
- [ ] Conclusion ties back to opening
- [ ] Tone is consistent
- [ ] No redundant sections
- [ ] CTA is clear
- [ ] Grammar and spelling checked
- [ ] Title is specific and engaging

## Citation Management

Support three formats (choose once, apply consistently):

**Inline:**
```
Studies show 40% productivity improvement (McKinsey, 2024).
```

**Numbered:**
```
Studies show 40% productivity improvement [1].
[1] McKinsey Global Institute. (2024). "The Economic Potential of Generative AI."
```

**Footnote:**
```
Studies show 40% productivity improvement^1
^1 McKinsey Global Institute. (2024). "The Economic Potential of Generative AI."
```

## Voice Preservation

The goal is to enhance, not replace your writing:
- Learn from samples you provide
- Suggest, don't replace
- Match your energy and tone
- Check periodically: "Does this sound like you?"
- Respect your preferences

## When to Use This Skill

- Writing articles with research backing
- Creating newsletters with citations
- Building thought leadership pieces
- Drafting technical tutorials
- Publishing case studies
- Any content-driven project requiring sources
